Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Pinkku ("we", "our", "the app") is a social media management tool that lets a business owner connect their own Facebook Page, Instagram, TikTok, Telegram, and Gmail accounts to manage posts and customer messages from one dashboard.

What we collect

  • Account information you provide when signing up (name, email, business name).
  • Access tokens and basic profile/Page information for any platform you choose to connect (e.g. your Facebook Page name, Page ID, and a Page access token), obtained only with your explicit permission through each platform's own login flow.
  • Messages sent to your connected Page/account by your customers, so they can be shown in your Customer DMs inbox and replied to.
  • Content you create in Pinkku (posts, captions, schedules).

How we use it

  • To publish posts to the platforms you connect, on your instruction.
  • To display incoming customer messages in your inbox and send the replies you write (or, if you enable it, an AI-drafted reply).
  • To detect dates/deadlines mentioned in messages and add them to your AI Smart Schedule.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or your customers' data to third parties.
  • We don't post or message anyone on your behalf without your action or an auto-reply setting you explicitly turned on.
  • We don't access platforms you haven't connected.

Data retention & deletion

Disconnecting a platform from Pinkku removes its stored access token immediately. To delete your Pinkku account and all associated data, contact us at the email below.

Third-party platforms

Connecting Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Telegram, or Google also means those platforms' own privacy policies apply to your use of them.
